
# Show/hide the _Issues panel_ (`⇧⌘I`)

The _Issues panel_ shows the list of issues associated with the edited stylesheet source. It can not be shown if there are no issue in the stylesheet source. When the _Issues panel_ is shown, all the stylesheet issues are highlighted. When it is hidden, the stylesheet returns to the editing style again.

## Show the _Issues panel_

To show the _Issues panel_:

- Click on the `Issues` button located at the center of the stylesheet tools panel below the style title panel.


## Hide the _Issues panel_

To hide the _Issues panel_:

- Click on the `Issues` button.
