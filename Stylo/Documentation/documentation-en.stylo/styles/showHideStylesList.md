
# Show/Hide _Styles List_ (`⇧⌘S`)

## Show the _Styles List_

Prerequisite: The styles list panel is not visible.

Do one of the following:

- Click on the right [sidebar button](#sidebarButtons) to show the [utility tools area](#utilityToolsArea) which contains the _styles list_.
- From the menu, choose`Styles`→`Show Styles`
- From the _Style Inspector_, click on the `Styles` button
- With the keyboard shortcut: `⇧⌘S`

## Hide the _Styles List_

Prerequisite: The styles list panel is visible.

Do one of the following:

- Click on the right [sidebar button](#sidebarButtons) to hide the [utility tools area](#utilityToolsArea) which contains the _styles list_.
- From the menu, choose `Styles`→`Hide styles`
- With the keyboard shortcut: `⇧⌘S`
