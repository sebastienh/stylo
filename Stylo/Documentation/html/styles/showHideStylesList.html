Show/Hide Styles List (⇧⌘S)

Show the Styles List

Prerequisite: The styles list panel is not visible.

Do one of the following:

  • Click on the right sidebar button to show the utility tools area which contains the styles list.
  • From the menu, chooseStyles→Show Styles
  • From the Style Inspector, click on the Styles button
  • With the keyboard shortcut: ⇧⌘S

Hide the Styles List

Prerequisite: The styles list panel is visible.

Do one of the following:

  • Click on the right sidebar button to hide the utility tools area which contains the styles list.
  • From the menu, choose Styles→Hide styles
  • With the keyboard shortcut: ⇧⌘S
